What's deliberately not in any table

A few generation-time traits drive the simulation — a product's popularity_weight & price elasticity, a merchant's category specialty, a customer's activity_weight & buyer segment — but none of them is a column. They're meant to be inferred from the resulting data later (the way a real analyst would), not read off a label.

popularity_weight / elasticityPer product. ~15% of products are deliberately price-insensitive or inverted — a known anomaly population.
merchant specialty / size tierPer merchant. Inferable later from what it actually ships in consignment_items.
activity_weight / segmentPer customer. Deal-seekers buy disproportionately in price-dip months.
foreign key derived, not a FK (price lookup)
